Down Payment Assistance Programs for Queens Buyers

Most first-time buyers in Queens qualify for at least one assistance program. Here are the main ones I help clients access:

FHA Loans — 3.5% Down Payment

The most popular program for first-time buyers. Minimum 3.5% down (vs. 20% conventional). Credit score minimum of 580. Gift funds from family allowed for the entire down payment. Available for houses, co-ops, and condos. For two-family homes, 75% of projected rental income counts toward your qualifying income.

SONYMA — Up to $15,000 Assistance

State of New York Mortgage Agency offers below-market interest rates plus up to $15,000 in down payment assistance grants. Income limits: approximately $100,000 to $150,000 depending on household size and county. Requires a homebuyer education course (available online). Can be combined with FHA financing for maximum benefit.

NYC HomeFirst — Up to $100,000

The city's flagship down payment and closing cost assistance program. Provides up to $100,000 for income-qualified first-time buyers purchasing in New York City. Forgivable over 10 to 15 years if you remain in the home. Income limits are more generous than most buyers expect — many middle-income Queens families qualify.

Conventional 3% Down

Some lenders offer conventional mortgages with as little as 3% down for first-time buyers with strong credit (720+). No mortgage insurance required on some programs. Better option for buyers with higher credit scores and stable W-2 income who want to avoid FHA's ongoing mortgage insurance premiums.

The First-Time Buyer Process — Start to Finish

  1. Consultation (free): We meet and I assess your readiness. If you're ready, we move to step 2. If not, I tell you exactly what to do first.
  2. Pre-approval (5-10 days): You apply with the lender I recommend. You receive a pre-approval letter stating your maximum purchase price and terms.
  3. Property search (2-8 weeks): I set up MLS alerts and we tour homes. I'll point out things you won't notice — condition issues, pricing errors, and hidden value.
  4. Offer and negotiation (1-3 days): I prepare a competitive offer based on comparable sales data. I handle negotiation with the seller's agent.
  5. Attorney review (1-2 weeks): Your attorney reviews the contract. I coordinate between your attorney and the seller's attorney.
  6. Inspection (1 week): I recommend trusted inspectors and attend the inspection with you. If issues arise, I negotiate repairs or credits.
  7. Mortgage commitment (3-5 weeks): Your loan moves through underwriting. I stay in contact with the lender to prevent delays.
  8. Closing (1 day): I'm at the closing table. We review every document together. You get your keys.

Total timeline: 60 to 120 days from consultation to closing for most first-time buyers.

FAQ — First-Time Buyer Consultation

What happens during the consultation?

We review your finances, identify loan programs you qualify for, discuss what your budget buys in different neighborhoods, and create a step-by-step action plan. 30-60 minutes, in person, phone, or video. Completely free.

What down payment assistance is available?

SONYMA offers up to $15,000 plus below-market rates. NYC HomeFirst provides up to $100,000 for income-qualified buyers. FHA requires only 3.5% down. Some lenders offer 3% down conventional loans. I help you identify which programs fit your situation.

How much money do I need for my first home?

With FHA and assistance programs, you could need as little as $10,000-$20,000 total for a co-op, or $45,000-$60,000 for a $700,000 two-family home. The exact amount depends on your loan program, purchase price, and which assistance you qualify for.

Does the consultation cost anything?

No. Completely free, no obligation. Buyer's agent commission is typically paid by the seller in New York. You pay nothing for my representation.
